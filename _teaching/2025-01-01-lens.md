title: "LENS: Log-Evidence-Grounded Neuro-Symbolic Analytics over 5G Log Traces"
collection: teaching
permalink: /teaching/lens
date: 2025-01-01
abstract: 'Question answering and analytics over telecom log systems, built on the premise that million-line 5G log traces should never be handed to an LLM directly: they exceed the context window, and attention alone does not reliably resolve the event pairing, identifier joins, grouping, and arithmetic that correct answers require. LENS is a log-evidence-grounded neuro-symbolic agent for natural-language analytics over 5G log traces. Offline, it decomposes raw logs into structured events stored in DuckDB and derives an empirical finite-state model from recurring event transitions, forming a Trace Analytics Ontology over event classes, extracted fields, system concepts, and analytical operations. At query time it synthesizes a query-specific analytics program that is structurally validated and then executed as analytical SQL. Because log traces can expose sensitive operational information, the design targets operator-controlled near-edge infrastructure where smaller open-weight models are deployable; experiments on a controlled 5G setting with a million-record trace show that richer data alone can reduce accuracy for smaller models, while coupling it with planning produces their best results. Advised by Prof. Dinesh Bharadia (UC San Diego) and Prof. Srinivas Shakkottai (Texas A&M).'
