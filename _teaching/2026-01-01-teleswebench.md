abstract: 'TeleSWEBench is the first commit-driven benchmark measuring how well automated software-engineering agents navigate and modify mathematically rigorous wireless stacks such as srsRAN 5G. Real developer commits from the srsRAN repository were distilled into 734 structured test cases with executable unit tests across three difficulty tiers (Easy, Medium, Difficult). The project also proposes TeleJudge, a hierarchical LLM-as-a-Judge framework that scores agent outputs at file level and aggregates verdicts holistically. Collaborators: Prof. Vijay K. Shah (NC State) and Prof. Dinesh Bharadia (UC San Diego).'
